# Danganronpa RPG - Student Brochure

*One page. Module 1.2.43 on Daggerheart. The names stay English: Hope, Despair, Sanity, Truth Bullet, Remnant, Blackened, Class Trial, Daily Life, Eclipse, Monokuma.*

**You are:** an Ultimate. Leg, Body, Hand, Eye, Shadow, Head. Health 4, Sanity 6, Hope 2 (max 6). Sanity at zero = **Breakdown** (disadvantage on every roll). Health at zero = **Wounded** (one action fewer). Rolls with Hope give you 1 Hope; rolls with Despair feed Monokuma; a critical gives 2 Hope.

## Each time of day (Morning, Noon, Afternoon, Evening, Night)

- **2 actions** and **1 free Move**. They refill when the next time of day begins.
- Moving inside your room is free. The first crossing into a connected room is your free Move; every further crossing costs 1 action. Drag your token - the cost lands when you arrive. Locked doors, sealed rooms, other people's bedrooms (no key) and Chained turn you back.
- Before each time of day comes the **Eclipse**: lights out, nobody sees anybody, you place your token up to 2 connected rooms away (any room before Night). Nothing spends an action but a Direct Murder, and no Calls.
- You see only your room and the people in it; visited rooms are veiled, the rest is fog. Voice follows your token - you hear only your room. Every room has search tokens (usually 3) shared by everybody.

## The actions (1 action each)

| Action | Roll | In short |
|---|---|---|
| **Search** | Eye / Hand | Name what you want. 8+ Tier 0, 12+ Tier 1, 18+ Tier 2, crit +1 tier. Spends a search token. Taking a weapon or a cleaning tool leaves a trace. |
| **Observe** | Eye | Copies a Remnant into your inventory as a Neutral Truth Bullet. A miss costs 1 Sanity. |
| **Analyze** | Head | Identify a Truth Bullet (a miss locks it until the chapter ends), ask the GM for a hint (14+ / 18+), or find a hidden stash (16+). |
| **Projects** | Hand / Body / Leg / Head | 12+ = +1 progress, 18+ = +2, crit = +2 and the action back. Propose new ones to the GM. Sabotage lives here too and always leaves a trace. |
| **Dynamic** | GM picks | Describe anything; the GM sets the difficulty. Gentler thresholds - the reward for inventing something. |
| **Rest** | - | Short: 1 action, pick 1, once per time of day. Long: 2 actions, pick 2, once per session. Sleep = Health, Meal = Sanity, Breath = Hope. Only in rooms marked for it. |
| **Listen** | Shadow | 14+ how many are next door, 18+ who, crit every adjacent room. |
| **Palm** | Hand + Shadow | Take (10+, unseen 15+) or plant (8+, unseen 13+) something in somebody's pocket. |
| **Tamper** | Shadow | Erase a trace you left (Hidden 9 to Obvious 18) or plant a misleading trail (15). Witnesses mean a cover roll (16) and Sanity if caught. |
| **Direct Murder** | GM | Only during an Eclipse, agreed with the GM, with the victim's player's consent. |

## Hope Calls at a glance

Support 1 · Experience 1 (GM) · Ultimate 1 (GM) · Contribution 2 · Sprint 2 · Reroll 3 · Resolve 3 · Burst 4 · Relief 4 · Loaded Die 6

Support = advantage for somebody in your room. Sprint = one free crossing. Burst = next action free. Relief = a Short Rest right now. Resolve = pick your own statistic. Loaded Die = one die set to 12.

## Things

3 usables; 2 gear slots shared by Murder Weapons, Cleaning Tools and Tools, only one of them stowed - **hold a tool ready or it does not count**. Truth Bullets and keys are unlimited. Despair on a roll wears the tool you used (Tier 0-1: 1 point, Tier 2: 2, Tier 3: 3). Broken things stay in your pocket until you stash or throw them away (that leaves a trace). Your bedroom is locked to others without a key; its stash holds 3 things and anyone in the room can rifle an open stash. Hand things over or share Truth Bullet copies with anyone in your room, free.

## The messenger and the GM

The button bottom-right opens **GM Chat** - one thread between you and every GM. Anything that needs a ruling (a specific Observe, a hint, a Dynamic action, a project proposal, Ultimate and Experience Calls) lands there next to your own messages. The Note tab holds your pre-session plans and boundaries.

## The safeword

Bottom-left of your sheet: **MISIUBOMBO** (or your table's word). Press it and the scene stops, the GMs are told, everybody sees the card. No reason needed, now or later.

## When it goes wrong

- **Murder.** A trap asks you to roll (Eye / Head vs 20) - being asked is the warning. A direct attack begins with your turn: Leave a clue (12), Secure a trace (15), Self-defence (18, unlocks Survive and Role reversal), use an item (15). Every turn costs Sanity, then Health. Walking in on one gives you one free choice: escape together, take a side, or look away.
- **Investigation.** Everyone gets the Autopsy Truth Bullet. Observe, Analyze, share. Three to five Key Remnants exist; together they narrow the suspects to 2-4. Search the body from its sheet. What you do not find, you will not have.
- **Class Trial.** Open discussion; Present Truth Bullets from your inventory. When the GM opens the Nonstop Debate, presenting is an **OBJECTION**: 60 s only you, then a 120 s rebuttal with the person you named. Then the secret vote - more than half of the ballots convicts, a tie counts as wrong. Right: the Blackened is executed, survivors Level Up. Wrong: the accused dies, the Blackened stays with a Reinforced Level Up.
- **Death.** Your Truth Bullets die with you, the rest stays on the body. After your trial you may join the GMs as a Monocub: Move and Confusion.
