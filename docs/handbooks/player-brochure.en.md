# Danganronpa RPG - Student Brochure

*One page. Module 1.2.112 on Daggerheart.*

**You are** an Ultimate: the one thing your character does best, written under their name on the sheet. Your six statistics are Leg, Body, Hand, Eye, Shadow, and Head.

You start with **Health 4, Sanity 6, and Hope 2** (Hope at most 6), and with 2 Experiences: short lines from your past on your sheet, worth +2 each when one applies to a roll.

| At zero | You are | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Sanity | **Breakdown** | disadvantage on every roll |
| Health | **Wounded** | 1 action fewer, outside the Class Trial |

A roll with Hope gives you 1 Hope, a critical (both dice the same) 2; a roll with Despair feeds the Despair pool of the Monokuma who watches you, unless your GM switched off **Rolls grant Despair**.

## Each time of day

- A day is five times of day: Morning, Noon, Afternoon, Evening, and Night. Each one opens with an **Eclipse**, and your **2 actions** and **1 Free Move** are refilled when it begins.
- Moving inside your room is free. Drag your token: your first crossing into a connected room is your Free Move, every further crossing costs 1 action, and the cost lands when you arrive.
- Locked doors, sealed rooms, other students' bedrooms (without a key), and Chained turn you back. Chained is a Despair Call that keeps you in your room until the time of day ends.
- In the Eclipse the lights are out, and nobody sees or hears anybody. You place your token up to 2 connected rooms away, or in any room in the Night Eclipse.
- You see only your room and the people in it; visited rooms are veiled, the rest is fog. If your table uses **Separate voice in each room**, your voice follows your token and you hear only your room.
- Every room has **Search Tokens**, usually 3, shared by everybody.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> During the Eclipse nothing spends an action but a killing you declare (a Direct Murder, or turning on the killer), and there are no Calls.

## The actions

Each action costs 1 action, except where its row says otherwise. A **Remnant** is what an act leaves on the map; a **Truth Bullet** is your copy of one, the evidence you carry. Things come in **Tiers** from 0 (a random, seemingly useless object) to 3 (made strictly for the job).

| Action | Roll | In short |
|---|---|---|
| **Search** | Eye, or another the GM approves | Name what you want: 8+ Tier 0, 12+ Tier 1, 18+ Tier 2, a critical 1 tier more. It spends a Search Token. Taking a Murder Weapon or a Cleaning Tool leaves a Remnant. |
| **Observe** | Eye | Copies a Remnant into your inventory as a Neutral Truth Bullet: one whose kind you do not know until it is analyzed. A miss costs 1 Sanity. |
| **Analyze** | Head | Identify a Truth Bullet (a miss locks it until the chapter ends), ask the GM for a hint (14+ / 18+), Find Stash (16+) or Find Passage (16+). |
| **Infiltrate** | Shadow, Leg, Body, or Hand | Listen (14+ how many are next door, 18+ who, a critical every neighboring room; rooms you have not been in stay unnamed: Unexplored room 1, 2...), Echoes, Tail Someone, Dash (12+ 2 free crossings, 18+ 3, a critical 4), Shut the Door, Force the Door, Pick the Lock, or Keep Watch. |
| **Palm** | Hand; Shadow to stay unseen | Take (12+, unseen 15+) something from somebody's pockets, or plant (8+, unseen 13+) one of your things there. Or see what they carry, which takes nothing: ask first, Body 12+ after a refusal; unseen Shadow 15+ then Hand 10+. |
| **Projects** | The project's | 12+ gives +1 progress, 18+ +2, a critical +2 and your action back. Propose new ones to the GM. Sabotage is here too and always leaves a Remnant. |
| **Facilities** | None, or the row's | Short Rest: 1 action, pick 1, once per time of day. Long Rest: 2 actions, pick 2, once per session. Sleep restores Health, Meal Sanity, Breath Hope; the rests work only in rooms marked for them. Below them is what your room offers. |
| **Dynamic Action** | You propose, the GM sets | Describe anything; the GM sets the difficulty. Gentler thresholds are the reward for inventing something. |
| **Tamper** | Shadow | Erase a Trace you have found in this room (Hidden 9 to Obvious 18) or Reshape a Trace (3 lower, and the GM approves the new words), neither on a Reinforced one; or plant a Misleading Trail (15). The attempt itself costs 1 action and no Sanity; with no action left, no Tamper. Witnesses mean a cover roll first (16), and being caught at it costs Sanity: 1 on a success with Despair or a failure, 2 on a failure with Despair. |
| **Direct Murder** | The GM rules | Only during an Eclipse, agreed with the GM, with the victim's player's consent. It is judged when the lights come up: you must then be <ins>alone with exactly one other person</ins>. |

|---|---|---|
| **Support** | 1 | Advantage for somebody in your room. |
| **Talent** | 1 | One of your Experiences added to your next roll, or your Ultimate's advantage on it, once the GM approves. |
| **Contribution** | 2 | +1 progress on a project in your room, no roll. |
| **Sprint** | 2 | One free crossing. |
| **Reroll** | 3 | Your last roll again, its action replayed. |
| **Resolve** | 2 | Pick any statistic yourself. |
| **Burst** | 4 | Your next action is free. |
| **Relief** | 4 | A Short Rest right now, in any room, without Breath. |
| **Loaded Die** | 6 | One die of your next action roll set to 12. |
| **Bribe** | 1 | Offer a Monocub 1 Hope for a favor; you pay only if they accept. |

## Things

**3 usables**; **2 gear slots** shared by Murder Weapons, Cleaning Tools, and Tools. Only one of the two may be stowed, so carrying two means one is in your hand. **Hold a tool ready or it does not count.** Truth Bullets and keys are unlimited.

> [!WARNING]
> Despair on a roll wears the tool you used by 1 point, and it breaks when its points run out (Tier 1: 1 point, Tier 2: 2, Tier 3: 3). A Tier 0 tool breaks on its first roll, whatever the roll. Broken things stay in your inventory until you stash them or throw them away; throwing away leaves a Remnant.

Your bedroom is locked to others without a key (a key opens it for whoever holds it, even a stolen one). Its stash holds 3 things, and it is open unless a hiding place has been built for it: anyone in the room can take one thing from an open stash, once each time of day, and you are told only that somebody has been in it. Hand things over or share Truth Bullet copies with anyone in your room, free.

## The messenger and the GM

The largest button in the bottom-right corner opens **GM Chat**: one thread between you and every GM, and there is no text channel between players. Anything that needs a ruling lands there: an Observe of a point of interest, a hint, a Dynamic Action, a project proposal, or a Talent Call. Its **Note** tab holds your pre-session plans and boundaries, such as whether you plan to kill, and whether you are open to dying.

| Button | Opens |
|---|---|
| The gear | **DRPG Settings**: language, theme, sounds, and voice. |
| The book | This brochure and the Player Handbook. |
| The stack of cards on a notice | The **Notice history**: the notices you were shown. |

## The safeword

The button at the bottom-left of your sheet shows your table's word: **Safe Word**, unless your table chose its own. The GM can change it.

> [!CAUTION]
> Press it and answer *Stop the scene?* with **Stop it** (Enter presses it): the scene stops, the GMs are told, and everybody sees the card. **Cancel** sends nothing. You need no reason, now or later.

## When it goes wrong

**Murder.** The **Blackened** is the student who killed. A trap asks you to roll (Eye or Head vs 20): being asked is the warning. A Direct Murder starts an incident, played in turns. If you are the victim, you go first, and from your second turn on every turn costs you 1 Sanity (2 alone with a trap), then Health once Sanity is gone.

| If you are the victim | Beat |
|---|---|
| **Leave a Clue** | 12 |
| **Secure a Trace** | 15 |
| **Self-Defense** (a hit opens Role Reversal, with Hope Survive too) | 18 |
| **Use an Item** | 15 |

Walking in on a Direct Murder gives you one choice: Escape Together (Leg 15, costing 1 Sanity, or 1 Health once your Sanity is gone), Double Role Reversal, Partners in Crime, or Averted Eyes, the last three free. A fourth person walking in cancels the incident: nobody dies, and the wounds stand.

**Investigation.** Everyone gets the Autopsy Truth Bullet; Observe, Analyze, share. Three to five Key Remnants exist; together they narrow the suspects to 2-4. You take what the body carries from its sheet with no action and one Hand roll for the lot: you also get a Truth Bullet of what you took, and the body keeps one Remnant of the looting, easier to find the lower you rolled. What nobody finds costs Despair, and Monokuma may reveal it at the trial.

**Class Trial.** It opens with a fresh set of actions, and nobody leaves the room; only Analyze stays open, plus Hope Calls and items. Discussion is open. **Present** shows a Truth Bullet from your inventory, free, with your Analyze result or without it, and everyone else gets a copy as shown; there is no Share in a trial.

- When the GM opens the Nonstop Debate, **OBJECTION!** on your voice tile interrupts whoever is speaking (or spoke last). You then have 30 s to choose your Truth Bullet, or the Objection is withdrawn and its price is not given back; then 60 s of you alone and a 120 s rebuttal with the person it was aimed at. An Objection, like Analyze in a trial, costs 1 action, else 1 Hope, else 1 Sanity.
- In Testimony and a Panic Round everyone speaks in turn (**Done**, **Pass**); in the Closing Argument the GM confirms one volunteer for 300 s. In these three only the speaker presents, and a Panic Round has no Objection.
- Then comes the secret vote: <ins>more than half of the ballots</ins> convicts, and a tie counts as wrong unless the table settles it. Right: the Blackened is executed when the trial ends, and every survivor takes a Level Up, a pick that makes the character stronger. Wrong: the accused is executed when the trial ends, and the Blackened stays with a Reinforced Level Up (3 picks), which waits for the class's next Level Up.

**Death.** Until somebody finds you, only the GMs and whoever was there know. Once the incident is over you talk with the Spectators and the other dead, and the living do not hear you. Once your death is known, your Truth Bullets die with you unless the GM keeps them, the rest stays on the body, and the GMs may invite you to play on as a Monocub if you accept the Monocub Rule: never tell the living what only the dead know. A Monocub moves freely and has Confusion (help or hinder a student's next roll), Redeem, Ask for Hope, Tinker (the same on a project), and Ghost Bet (a bet on the verdict); a living student may offer you a Bribe.
