# Danganronpa RPG - Student Brochure

*One page. Module 1.2.112 on Daggerheart.*

**You are** an Ultimate: Leg, Body, Hand, Eye, Shadow and Head. You start with **Health 4, Sanity 6 and Hope 2** (Hope at most 6).

| At zero | You are | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Sanity | **Breakdown** | disadvantage on every roll |
| Health | **Wounded** | 1 action fewer, outside the Class Trial |

A roll with Hope gives you 1 Hope, a critical 2; a roll with Despair feeds Monokuma.

## Each time of day

- You get **2 actions** and **1 Free Move**. They refill when the Eclipse before the next time of day begins.
- Moving inside your room is free. Drag your token: your first crossing into a connected room is your Free Move, every further crossing costs 1 action, and the cost lands when you arrive. Locked doors, sealed rooms, other people's bedrooms (without a key) and Chained turn you back; Chained is a Despair Call that keeps you in your room until the time of day ends.
- Before each time of day comes the **Eclipse**: lights out, and nobody sees or hears anybody. You place your token up to 2 connected rooms away, or in any room in the Eclipse before Night.
- You see only your room and the people in it; visited rooms are veiled, the rest is fog. If your table uses regional voice, your voice follows your token and you hear only your room.
- Every room has **Search Tokens**, usually 3, shared by everybody.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> During the Eclipse nothing spends an action but a Direct Murder, and there are no Calls.

## The actions (1 action each)

| Action | Roll | In short |
|---|---|---|
| **Search** | Eye, or another the GM approves | Name what you want: 8+ Tier 0, 12+ Tier 1, 18+ Tier 2, a critical 1 tier more. It spends a Search Token. Taking a Murder Weapon or a Cleaning Tool leaves a Remnant. |
| **Observe** | Eye | Copies a Remnant into your inventory as a Neutral Truth Bullet. A miss costs 1 Sanity. |
| **Analyze** | Head | Identify a Truth Bullet (a miss locks it until the chapter ends), ask the GM for a hint (14+ / 18+), Find Stash (16+) or Find Passage (16+). |
| **Infiltrate** | Shadow, Leg, Body or Hand | Listen (14+ how many are next door, 18+ who, a critical every adjacent room; rooms you have not been in stay unnamed: Unexplored room 1, 2...), Echoes, Tail Someone, Dash (12+ 2 free crossings, 18+ 3, a critical 4), Shut the Door, Force the Door, Pick the Lock or Keep Watch. |
| **Palm** | Hand; Shadow to stay unseen | Take (12+, unseen 15+) something from somebody's pockets, or plant (8+, unseen 13+) one of your things there. Or see what they carry: ask first, Body 12+ after a refusal; unseen Shadow 15+ then Hand 10+. |
| **Projects** | The project's | 12+ gives +1 progress, 18+ +2, a critical +2 and your action back. Propose new ones to the GM. Sabotage is here too and always leaves a Remnant. |
| **Facilities** | None, or the row's | Short Rest: 1 action, pick 1, once per time of day. Long Rest: 2 actions, pick 2, once per session. Sleep restores Health, Meal Sanity, Breath Hope; the rests work only in rooms marked for them. Below them is what your room offers. |
| **Dynamic Action** | You propose, the GM sets | Describe anything; the GM sets the difficulty. Gentler thresholds are the reward for inventing something. |
| **Tamper** | Shadow | Erase a Trace you have found in this room (Hidden 9 to Obvious 18) or Reshape a Trace (3 lower, and the GM approves the new words), neither on a Reinforced one; or plant a Misleading Trail (15). It costs 1 action and no Sanity; with no action left, no Tamper. Witnesses mean a cover roll (16), which can cost Sanity even when it works: 1 on Despair, 1-2 if you are caught. |
| **Direct Murder** | The GM rules | Only during an Eclipse, agreed with the GM, with the victim's player's consent. It is judged when the lights come up: you must then be <ins>alone with exactly one other person</ins>. |

## Things

**3 usables**; **2 gear slots** shared by Murder Weapons, Cleaning Tools and Tools, only one of them stowed. **Hold a tool ready or it does not count.** Truth Bullets and keys are unlimited.

> [!WARNING]
> Despair on a roll wears the tool you used by 1 point, and it breaks when its points run out (Tier 1: 1 point, Tier 2: 2, Tier 3: 3). A Tier 0 tool breaks on its first roll, whatever the roll. Broken things stay in your pocket until you stash them or throw them away; throwing away leaves a Remnant.

Your bedroom is locked to others without a key (a key opens it for whoever holds it, even a stolen one); its stash holds 3 things, and anyone in the room can rifle an open stash. Hand things over or share Truth Bullet copies with anyone in your room, free.

## The messenger and the GM

The largest of the three buttons in the bottom-right corner, the one on top, opens **GM Chat**: one thread between you and every GM. Anything that needs a ruling lands there next to your own messages: a specific Observe, a hint, a Dynamic Action, a project proposal or a Talent Call. Its **Note** tab holds your pre-session plans and boundaries.

| Button | Opens |
|---|---|
| The gear, bottom-right | **DRPG Settings**: Language (English / Polski, this browser only), theme, scale, sounds and voice (microphone, output, push to talk), reduced motion and high contrast. |
| The book, under the messenger, right of the gear | This brochure and the Player Handbook, in the language you picked for the module. |
| The stack of cards, bottom-right corner of the notice tile | The **Notice history**: the notices this browser showed you, the newest 250 at most, until a season reset clears them. |

> [!TIP]
> **Clear** in the Notice history empties it: before you share your screen, and on a shared computer before you hand it over.

## The safeword

The button at the bottom-left of your sheet shows your table's word: **Safe Word**, unless your table chose its own. The GM can change it.

> [!CAUTION]
> Press it and answer *Stop the scene?* with **Stop it** (Enter presses it): the scene stops, the GMs are told and everybody sees the card. **Cancel** sends nothing. You need no reason, now or later.

## When it goes wrong

**Murder.** A trap asks you to roll (Eye or Head vs 20): being asked is the warning. In a Direct Murder the incident begins with your turn, and every turn after your first costs you Sanity, then Health. Walking in on an incident gives you one free choice: escape together, take a side, or look away.

| Your choice in the incident | Beat |
|---|---|
| **Leave a Clue** | 12 |
| **Secure a Trace** | 15 |
| **Self-Defense** (unlocks Survive and Role Reversal) | 18 |
| **Use an Item** | 15 |

**Investigation.** Everyone gets the Autopsy Truth Bullet; Observe, Analyze, share. Three to five Key Remnants exist; together they narrow the suspects to 2-4. You take what the body carries from its sheet with no action and one Hand roll for the lot: you also get a Truth Bullet of what you took, and the body keeps one Remnant of the looting, easier to find the lower you rolled. What nobody finds costs Despair, and Monokuma may reveal it at the trial.

**Class Trial.** It opens with a fresh set of actions, and nobody leaves the room; only Analyze stays open, plus Hope Calls and items. Discussion is open. **Present** shows a Truth Bullet from your inventory, free, with your Analyze result or without it, and everyone else gets a copy as shown; there is no Share in a trial.

- When the GM opens the Nonstop Debate, **OBJECTION!** on your voice tile interrupts whoever is speaking (or spoke last). You then have 30 s to choose your Truth Bullet, or the Objection is withdrawn and its price is not given back; then 60 s of you alone and a 120 s rebuttal with the person it was aimed at. An Objection, like Analyze in a trial, costs 1 action, else 1 Hope, else 1 Sanity.
- In Testimony and a Panic Round everyone speaks in turn (**Done**, **Pass**); in the Closing Argument the GM confirms one volunteer for 300 s. In these three only the speaker presents, and a Panic Round has no Objection.
- Then comes the secret vote: <ins>more than half of the ballots</ins> convicts, and a tie counts as wrong. Right: the Blackened is executed when the trial ends, survivors Level Up. Wrong: the accused dies when the trial ends, the Blackened stays with a Reinforced Level Up, which waits for the class's next Level Up.

**Death.** Until somebody finds you, only the GMs and whoever was there know. Once the incident is over you talk with the Spectators and the other dead, and the living do not hear you. Once your death is known, your Truth Bullets die with you, the rest stays on the body, and the GMs may invite you to play on as a Monocub if you accept the Monocub Rule: never tell the living what only the dead know. A Monocub moves freely and has Confusion, Redeem, Ask for Hope, Tinker and Ghost Bet; a living student may offer you a Bribe.
