## 1. Goal

The audit's goal, translated: "A change of the time of day and of the phase (Daily Life, night, Investigation, Class
Trial, Eclipse) has a short animated transition in the curtain and the HUD instead of a sudden jump. Each transition
lasts under about 1.5 s, can be skipped, is static under reduced motion and does not delay the state change itself."

Why (translated): the owner's request of 26.09, the third item of attractiveness in the audit. "After E17 and E37 the
change of the clock's state and of the Eclipse goes one way, so the transition hangs on one event; after E48-E49 it uses
the ready colour tokens and the curtain."

The owner's two asks: "animated phase transitions" (26.09); "death puts the curtain out down to bare glass, a Monocub
brings it back, the discovery of a body flickers the edges and the pulse" (10.10).

Done when (translated): "Every change of time of day and of phase has a transition in both themes, skippable, static
under reduced motion, with no effect on the time of the state change, and measured in E53's budget."

Verify (translated): "Test R: the transition follows from one phase-change event and changes no state; under reduced
motion no animation; two changes closer together than the transition end in the correct state. Glass harness at several
sizes during the transition. E53's budget includes the transition."

Audit effort: 3, raised to about 4 on 10.10, then +1-1.5 (UI following the curtain) and +0.5-1 (textures) on 10.11 (each
labelled an estimate there, not a measurement). The audit has no decision records for E62 (`decisions: {}`); the owner's

## 3. What exists now

Read in the code (file:line at f22a7e8):

- **The one road of a clock change.** sync.mjs `KIND_REFRESH[SYNC.clock]` runs `assembly, bar, hud, sheets, eclipse`,
  the visibility pass, then `timeOfDayHook`, `phaseHook`, `eclipseHook` each only when its part moved (sync.mjs:474-475);
  the hooks are `drpgTimeOfDayChanged`, `drpgPhaseChanged`, `drpgEclipseChanged` (sync.mjs:449-451). The HUD's render
  writes `body.dataset.drpgPhase` and `body.dataset.drpgTime` (hud.mjs:522, 526).
- **The curtain hears the body, not the hooks.** glass.mjs `registerGlass` observes the body's `data-drpg-phase`,
  `data-drpg-time` and `class` (glass.mjs:4760); `onStateChange` debounces 60 ms, compares each curtain's accent
  (`resolveAcc`) and calls `turning()` and `rebuild()` (glass.mjs:4567-4578). `rebuildAll` snapshots the old panes and
  look and hands them to `glassTurn` (glass.mjs:4273-4316). So the curtain does not know which clock change it is turning
  for, only that its accent moved; E62's direction per transition needs that knowledge (inferred: the observer can take
  `attributeOldValue`, or C1 passes the kind from the clock's hooks).
- **The turn (E23 C1b, E96 C2), built for E61 and E62.** glass.mjs:3680-3698 says "The entry point is `glassTurn`, for
  E61's and E62's sequences as well as for this file's own turns". `glassTurn(...)` (glass.mjs:4103) takes `from`,
  `look`, `to`, the two accents, `move`, `flash`, `dur`, `curve`, `frame`; it cancels a running turn first
  (`job.turn?.cancel()`, glass.mjs:4106), which is "cut, not queued" already (R1412). `turnPlan` (glass.mjs:3795) orders
  batches from the centre outward only (`centreOrder`), `TURN_BATCHES = 8`, `TURN_FADE = 300`, `TURN_MOVE = 700`
  (glass.mjs:3710). The flash at today's brightness is the frozen table `FLASH` (420 ms, glass.mjs:3720). A recolour is
  a per-batch crossfade (glass.mjs:4171-4200). Reduced motion: `turnPlan(..., reduced: REDUCED())` answers `none`
  (glass.mjs:3797) and a turn running is cancelled when the class arrives (glass.mjs:4242-4243, R1413).
- **What does not exist.** No batch order other than centre-out; no random groups with uneven gaps; no fade to bare
  glass and no flicker (`git grep -n -i "dead\|deceas\|death" scripts/glass.mjs` finds nothing of the kind); no per-frame
  following of the UI blocks during a recut (they take the new colour by CSS transition on `data-drpg-turning`,
  stained-glass.css:1072-1092, not their geometry); no Esc skip of a turn (`git grep '"Escape"' scripts` finds four
  handlers, none in glass.mjs).
- **Motion tokens.** motion.css: `--drpg-t-turn` 840 ms, `--drpg-t-curtain` 1200 ms (since E23 C1b), `--drpg-t-eclipse`
  1400 ms (motion.css:64-86); all zeroed by `prefers-reduced-motion` and by `body.drpg-reduced-motion`
  (motion.css:117-151). motion.mjs `reducedMotion()` reads the class then the media query (motion.mjs:135); glass.mjs
  has its own `REDUCED()` and `pulseOn()` (glass.mjs:3436-3439). The client settings are `SETTINGS.reducedMotion` and
  `SETTINGS.glassPulse` (settings.mjs:1509, 1559); pulse off puts `drpg-no-pulse` on the body (settings.mjs:4013).
- **The HUD.** hud.mjs `slideTimeOfDay` slides the time label on `TURN()`, mirrored on a rewind, zero under reduced
  motion (hud.mjs:897-960). The HUD's border and shadow transition on the turn token and its filter on the Eclipse token
  (danganronpa.css:1751-1755). The board's Eclipse drain is a CSS filter transition (danganronpa.css:1698-1700).
  No other HUD animation on a phase change was found (`git grep "TURN()" scripts/hud.mjs`: one call).
- **Legacy.** Monokuma Legacy has no curtain; MK-R3 draws its E62-3 as four frames (HUD, status, Despair bar, notice
  tile) flashing at the same gaps (MK-R3 index.html, "W Legacy błyskają 4 ramki").
- **The day summary.** day-summary.mjs listens on all three clock hooks (day-summary.mjs:70-72) and raises
  `showPopup(content, { title, sticky: true, source: "summary" })` (day-summary.mjs:255), kind left at its default.
  popup.mjs `hostFor` sends only sticky `evidence` and `objection` to the centre stage (popup.mjs:231-235), so the summary
  stands in the corner today, not the centre. A page whose clock is hidden by an incident shows none
  (day-summary.mjs:87).
- **The body's card.** chapter.mjs `runDiscovery` posts a public card with `sfx: { key: "bodyFound", gm: true }`,
  `popupKind: "objection"` and a title (chapter.mjs:1098-1103); popup.mjs's card route passes no `sticky`
  (popup.mjs:1265-1272), so this card also lands in the corner. `drpgBodyAnnounced` is called by `callBodyAnnounced`
  (chapter.mjs:1345), and R3145 names E62 as one of its listeners (tests-tier0.mjs:22344).
- **The sounds.** `SFX_EVENTS` (config.mjs:4645) holds 55 rows (counted with a grep of the row shape over config.mjs
  4645-5100; to recount in the spec). `bodyFound` (config.mjs:4838), `death`, `monocub` (config.mjs:4858) exist; the
  Monocub sound plays on the actor flag for the owner and GMs (sfx.mjs:313-317). `playSfx` returns a boolean, not the
  sound, so the length of the `bodyFound` file is not known to the caller (sfx-play.mjs:455-479); the cooldown is 80 ms
  (sfx-play.mjs:52), under the shortest flash gap of 360 ms.
- **Deaths on the victim's client.** settings.mjs `pendingDeath` reads the player's own copy `readMine("deaths")`
  (settings.mjs:3286-3292); `onDeathsChange` calls `drpgDeathsChanged` (settings.mjs:2967-2971); incident-store.mjs
  `knowsOfDeath` lets an owner of the body know (incident-store.mjs:1206-1211). monocub.mjs `setMonocub`
  (monocub.mjs:116) writes the flag. The old E67 ("the event of becoming a Monocub") is not a row of tools/stages.json;
  1.2.101's notes say E95 took over E72's early part and "E62, E68, E64 and E69 read what it takes over from the old E67"
  (.github/release-notes/v1.2.101.md:249-250).
- **The notice history.** popup.mjs documents the interface "every later recorder takes (E18, E50, E62 and E70 after
  E81)" (popup.mjs:710-712); R479 holds that comment naming E62 (tests-tier0.mjs:14034).
- **Counters.** perf-counters.mjs counts `glass.turn`, `glass.turn.layers`, `glass.turn.recut` (perf-counters.mjs:63),
  read by `game.drpg.counters()`, `fps()` and scenario 86, which moves the clock (86-frame-work.mjs:395) and starts and
  ends an Eclipse (86-frame-work.mjs:436-444).
- **The glass harness.** `drpgTurn(kind)` has four kinds: phase-recut, phase, hour, layout (glass-harness.html:1657-1779;
  glass-sweep.mjs:979-1010); `--turn` reads clock, texture (`texmetric`, the mean absolute Laplacian of luminance),
  flash and still frames. The default size list of glass-sweep.mjs has 25 sizes (glass-sweep.mjs:268-269, counted);
  the owner's 10.11 text says 22 and the root rules file says 23 for the sweep: the spec picks the list and says which.

## 7. Risks

- **The UI following a recut (C6).** Nothing follows geometry per frame today; the blocks are Foundry and module DOM
  whose layout the curtain measures (`curtainGeometry`). Animating them by transform from the pane's affine map, without
  a reflow per frame, is new work, and the self-check must stay clean in every frame at every size. The owner's own
  estimate was +1-1.5.
- **Textures (C7).** E23 and E96 each fixed a cause; the owner still saw it at 1.2.103. The cause has to be measured
  before the fix, or the third fix repeats the first two.
- **Flash safety.** Three flashes a second is the WCAG 2.3.1 count; the area and the red-flash rules also apply. A
  group's screen share must be measured at the smallest sizes, where a group of the same panes covers more.
- **Secrecy.** The death fade runs from a death not yet public. It reads only the victim's own copy, so it tells nobody
  else anything, but a GM's screen-share or a shared monitor shows it: the owner accepted that as the table's decision
  (10.10, 14:05).
- **Two effects at once.** A death in an incident, the discovery flicker, the Investigation flashes and an Eclipse can
  land within seconds; each new turn cancels the last (R1412), so the planner must decide which end state wins.
- **Esc.** Foundry binds Esc to closing windows and the main menu; the skip must not take it from them.
- **The harness.** jsdom has no layout, canvas or Web Animations timing: C1-C5's tests run on stand-in jobs (as
  R1411-R1413 do); every visual claim is the glass sweep's in Chromium or a LIVE row.
