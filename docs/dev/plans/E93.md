> (1) The Rest tile becomes the Facilities tile: inside it Short rest, Long rest and the actions available in the room the
> character stands in. (2) Two sources of room actions: predefined by the GM in Room Setup (they need a roll, like other

- Scenarios that read this code: 40-flow, 60-ledger, 83-roll-integrity (Rest); 33-bridge-paths, 71-sheet (Overflow rules);

## 3. Commits

**C0 - spec, effect census, red tests.** One row per effect of goal (5): what it writes, who writes it (the GM, always),
how it expires, and whether Daggerheart holds it. Red: a second person uses the GM's action in the same time of day; a
GM's action with no roll; a project's action after its lifetime; a 0-cost action takes an action; each new Overflow
effect off. R numbers: about 8.

**C1 - the data.** Room actions per room in a GM store or region flags (the GM's predefined actions are not secret once
in the room; their text may be: Q-E93-2), each `{ id, label, trait, threshold, effects[], costsAction }`; a project's
`lifetime: { kind: "forever" | "days" | "timesOfDay", n }` and `completedAt` in its meta; the season and chapter
clean-ups drop expired ones. World-secrets rules for any new world field. R: about 4.

**C2 - Room Setup.** The Rest tab becomes Facilities: the two rest flags as today, plus the room's actions editor (kit
`dataTable`, `stepper`, `toggle`). Lang keys EN and PL; glossary entry "Facilities" (English in Polish, like other tile
names, D33a). R: about 3. Scenarios: 66-rooms, 74-room-tile, 76-passages. Screenshots: both themes, 1920 and 1280.

**C3 - the bridge.** New actions per the trust-model rules file's five steps: `facility.use` (`knownSender`, `owns`,
the room read on the GM, the limit read on the GM, the roll named in `rolled` for a GM's action), with effects applied
on the primary GM only; the use's trace placed by the GM. The limits: a GM's action closed for all after one use (the
GMs' store row per room, action and time of day); a project's action once per person. This is a road a player's packet
reaches: mark it for mutants. R: about 7. Scenarios: 33-bridge-paths (legal control), 30-security (a claimed room,
a reused action), 83-roll-integrity (the roll), 62-investigation (the trace).

**C4 - the tile.** The Rest tile renamed Facilities; its window lists Short rest, Long rest, the room's GM actions and the
completed projects' actions with source and time left; the Projects window shows the lifetime. R: about 3. Scenarios:
40-flow, 60-ledger, 74-room-tile, 88-flow-length. Screenshots: tile and window, both themes, 1920, 1280, narrow.

**C5 - temporary effects.** Expiry at the next time of day through the clock's boundary (clock.mjs `advanceTimeOfDay`
:283), a stacking rule (Q-E93-3). R: about 4.

**C6 - Overflow.** Five rows in `OVERFLOW.effects` (lockdown, insomnia, toll, jitters, powercut), `kind: "state"`, each
with its reader in the Facilities road; switches in `overflowSection`. R: about 5. Scenarios: 33-bridge-paths, 71-sheet,
40-flow. Screenshot: Despair Flow's table, both themes.

**C7 - the scenario and the books.** Four players: GM and project actions in one room, the trace in the investigation,
Lockdown and Power cut. GM and player handbooks EN and PL. R: about 2.

Total new R numbers: about 36. `touches`: ["sockets", "rolls"].

## 6. Risks

- The effect list is wide; each effect is a write on the GM with its own reason in `WRITE_REASONS`. Many small roads, each a
  place a player's packet could claim more; R1b and R220 must hold every one.
- Expiry with the time of day touches the boundary's take-back (clock.mjs `TAKE_BACK_MS`): a rewind must restore what expired.
- A lifetime on projects touches season and chapter clean-ups (`clearSeasonProjects`, `sealOldRepairs`).
- E69 (the Infiltrate tile, next) changes the same tile grid.
