## 1. Goal

The audit's `goal` (stage E73), translated and shortened:

> Hall zones on E70's mode engine: every participant stands with their token in one of two zones of the fixed hall, and
> the zone is an answer or a camp. (0) Picking zones: two region selects in the Scenes tab, "Zone A" and "Zone B" (PL
> "Strefa A", "Strefa B"), stored in `sceneRoles` as `{ trial, zones: { a, b } }` with no migration; a warning when the
> hall has no zones, both name one region, the zones do not cover the whole scene or overlap. (1) A new trial-zones.mjs:
> `zoneRegions(scene)`, `sidesNow()` counted on every client, one token per player, no Monocubs, no muted dead; a
> participant with no token in the hall gets a side-pick window. (2) Straw Poll: 45 s, 2/3 gives a Finding "The class
> thinks: ...", below it "The class is split: A 4, B 3" and a Scrum hint. (3) Logic Dive: sets of 1-5 questions, the truth
> in the GM store; simple majority; a tie is always wrong and ends the set without revealing the truth; replayable with a
> GM-only warning. (4) Scrum Debate: camps in turn, 2 x the larger camp's turns, 20-40 s a turn, an optional thesis.
> (5) Findings in `trialProgress.findings`. (6) The GM's bar: Count now, Skip question, -30 s, +30 s, Skip, End mode, with
> `game.drpg` functions. (7) Music, sounds, texts and handbooks.

Done when (audit `doneWhen`): the GM picks two zones and is warned when they are missing, repeat, overlap or leave a
gap; the three modes work in the hall with and without zones, in both themes and languages; no true answer reaches world
data before it is judged; a tie always ends Logic Dive unrevealed; a set can be replayed with a GM warning; every GM
intervention is public and has an API function; Findings are on the vote's opening card; each mode has a handbook entry
and a behaviour test; zones do nothing outside the three modes, and inside one they are drawn like discovered rooms,
hide no token, are named after the mode's current options, and an entry plays the room's outline and `zoneEntered`.

## 6. Risks

- High: new play with no table rehearsal (D26 = 3).
- The truth must not reach world data before the judgement: GM store only, tier 0 shape guard, canary seed.
- v14 regions with holes and Levels: `testPoint` fallback, live check 01.
- Live counts on every client must fit the render budget (81-render-budget's steps).
- The console rebuild touches every trial scenario; doing it in the same stage as three new modes is the largest
  single risk of the stage.
- Stands lie in the zones: a player who does not move answers with their stand's zone; the GM handbook says so.
