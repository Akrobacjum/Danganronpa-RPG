# E92 - Spectator, a new kind of player (1.2.109)

Plan written 12.11.2026 by the planning line, on the coordinator's release line at commit f22a7e8 (1.2.105 in progress). Documents only.

## 1. Goal

The owner's audit entry (E92 `goal`, 04.11, translated):

> A new kind of player account, the Spectator: the GM assigns it in the Students window. (1) Its token is invisible to
> players (the GM sees it as a hidden Foundry token). (2) Always muted: the microphone is blocked in every phase, but it
> hears the voice channel of the room its token stands in (in an Eclipse and at the trial by the same `voiceTargets`
> branches as the living, listen only). (3) Ghost mode: it passes through walls and closed doors, no movement limit and
> no fees; its movement reveals no rooms, leaves no traces and triggers no traps and no body discovery. (4) It sees the
> whole map the players have discovered (the discovery ledger without the Mastermind's row) and every character token
> whatever the room, but nothing secret (secret projects, true trace types, who the Mastermind is, the GMs' secrets).
> (5) It is not a student: it counts in no vote, trial cast, assembly, Despair, actions or tiles; sheet and HUD in a
> spectator mode.

Done when (audit `doneWhen`): the Spectator works from its assignment in the Students window to the season's end, the EN
and PL handbooks describe it, and every student list and every secrecy gate has a test with a Spectator.

## 2. What exists now (read at f22a7e8)

- **Roles.** The Students window has three roles, `ROLES = ["student", "monokuma", "npc"]` (scripts/gm-students.mjs:205),
  `roleOf` (:210) and `changeRole` (:215), each role a flag written only by a GM's road. `studentActors`
  (scripts/monokuma.mjs:68) leaves out `isMonokuma` and `isNotInClass` (:31, :44); its header says the roster is read by
  71 calls in 19 files. Measured now: 51 calls of `studentActors(` in scripts/ outside the test files. The flag
  `notInClass` is in `FLAGS` (scripts/config.mjs:245) and in the audit's `GM_FLAGS` (scripts/sheet-audit.mjs:161), so a
  player's own write of it is put back. A `spectator` flag can follow the same three places.
- **Reads that skip the roster.** `git grep 'type === "character"' scripts` outside tests: 73 lines in 35 files. Each
  is a place a Spectator's actor would be counted as a character; the census in C1 classifies every one.
- **Voice.** `placeVoices` (scripts/voice.mjs:913) already gives a connected account with no character a row "main room"
  (:954, comment names "a spectator"); a Spectator with a token would instead get its token's room through `voiceClaim`
  (:1515). `micHold` (:1130) returns `{ reason, held }`, read by voice-tile.mjs `micState` (:910); the trial's block writes
  Foundry's `permissions.BROADCAST_AUDIO` on the user (voice.mjs:1216, `broadcastAudioKnown` :1327; the GM's block
  voice-tile.mjs:1278). The client holds one LiveKit room at a time: `applyBreakout` (voice-client.mjs:521) sets the room
  then disconnects or reconnects (:568-569). That one connection cannot be in two rooms is my reading of this code; I did
  not read avclient-livekit's source.
- **Visibility.** `tokenShownTo` (scripts/visibility.mjs:507): GM null (all), Eclipse own-only, then the room rule, with
  the Mastermind's lair as the one "see all" for a non-GM. Remnant tokens go through their own gate
  (`applyToRemnantToken`, named in the comment there). Every browser holds every token (the trust model's own
  statement about Listen), so hiding the Spectator's token from players is display, not secrecy.
- **Fog.** The ledger is a GM store (`discoveryStore`); a player gets only its own rows through the `fog` copy
  (scripts/gm-stores.mjs:1446; settings.mjs `discoveryLedger` :3003). `ledgerRooms` (fog.mjs:1437) skips the Mastermind's
  row on a GM's browser; `recordDiscovery` (fog.mjs:1505) writes on the primary GM only. A Spectator needs a new
  projection of the store, computed on the GM: the union without the Mastermind's row.
- **Movement.** `crossingVerdict` (movement.mjs:1603) lets a Monokuma through at its top (:1605); the trial lock (:1633)
  and the incident lock come after. A GM's put-back writes with `constrainOptions: { ignoreWalls: true }` (:1353). Whether
  Foundry v14 lets a player's own drag pass a wall is Foundry's; not measured here (LIVE-E92-02).
- **Chat.** `git grep -n "renderChatLog" scripts` outside tests finds nothing, and the four `ui.chat` reads
  (private-rolls.mjs:1889, secret.mjs:1314, :1334, :1477) update or read messages; none hides the log for a user. That
  a public message still reaches the Spectator's browser is Foundry's behaviour, inferred, not measured here.

## 3. Commits

**C0 - spec, census and red tests.** The census of the 73 `type === "character"` lines and 51 roster calls, each marked
"roster", "a Spectator is a character here" or "must leave it out". Red tests: a Spectator counted in the vote, the trial
cast, an assembly, Despair's donors; its token shown to a player; its microphone open; its move stopped by a wall; a
secret project's token shown to it. R numbers: about 10.

**C1 - the role.** `FLAGS.spectator`, `isSpectator`, a fourth role in `ROLES` and `ROLE_WORDS`, `studentActors` and
`studentCensus` leave it out, `GM_FLAGS` gains it, world-secrets.mjs has a rule if any field is added (a flag naming who
watches is not a secret; say so beside the rule). New lang keys EN and PL (glossary entry first: the Polish
"Obserwator" is already Foundry's OBSERVER permission level in lang/pl.json:4594-4603, so the role needs another
Polish word; Q-E92-2). R: about 4. Scenarios that read the touched code: 30-security, 71-sheet (notInClass), 62, 63, 83
(studentActors). Screenshot: the Students window's role select, both themes, 1920 and 1280.

**C2 - voice.** `micHold` gains reason `spectator` (held: true) in every phase; the account's `BROADCAST_AUDIO` is
blocked as the trial's block is and recorded the same way so an exit puts back exactly what was there; `placeVoices`
places the Spectator by its token as the living, never as a witness and never in `held`'s reasoning. R: about 4.
Scenarios: 17-assistant, 41-trial-scene, 75-voice-tile (voiceTargets, micHold), 30-security, 63 (BROADCAST_AUDIO).

**C3 - ghost movement and the token.** `crossingVerdict` passes a Spectator like a Monokuma, before the trial and
incident locks; the GM writes the landing with `ignoreWalls` (as :1353) through a new bridge action (the trust-model
rules file's five steps: `spectator.move`, `knownSender`, `owns`, a run that checks `isSpectator` as the GMs hold it).
No `recordDiscovery`, no crossing record, no trap, no sighting, no body discovery for it. Token `hidden` for players,
greyed with a darker outline on the GM (styles in components.css; no breakpoint). R: about 6. Scenarios: 66-rooms,
76-passages, 60-ledger, 10-murder, 13-murder-signals, 33-bridge-paths (legal control), 30-security (a player's packet
claiming spectator). Screenshot: GM canvas with the token, both themes.

**C4 - what it sees.** A new GM-store copy (`from: "spectator"`): the discovery union without the Mastermind's row,
sent only to Spectator accounts; `tokenShownTo` answers "see all characters" for a Spectator, Remnant tokens keep their
gate; a secret project's token and tray row stay closed (`canSee`, projects-secrecy.mjs:201). R: about 6. Scenarios:
60-ledger, 17-assistant (discoveryLedger), 72-canary (a Spectator's browser added to its scan; zero hits wanted).

**C5 - sheet, HUD, tiles, chat.** Spectator mode: no action grid, no Hope, no tiles, a HUD line "watching"; the chat
sidebar hidden on its browser. R: about 3. Scenarios: 71-sheet, 84-viewports. Screenshots: sheet and HUD, both themes,
1920, 1280 and a narrow width.

**C6 - Spectators' room.** If LIVE-E92-01 confirms one room per connection: a "Spectator talk" switch moves the account to
`drpg-spectators`, mic open there only, back to the token's room on the switch. R: about 3. Scenario: 75-voice-tile.

**C7 - E61, E62, E94 and the books.** What a Spectator sees at the trial entry and cut-in, the day boards and phase
transitions; a scenario with four players and a Spectator (voice room, walls, map); handbooks EN and PL. R: about 3.

Total new R numbers: about 39 (a count; the coordinator assigns them). `touches`: ["sockets", "scenes"] for the
stages.json row (a bridge action and token moves), so the local gate is required.

## 6. Risks

- R1: a list that reads `type === "character"` directly keeps counting the Spectator; the C0 census is the guard.
- R2: the discovery copy for a Spectator is a new road out of a GM store; a mistake there names the Mastermind's walks.
  72-canary on a Spectator browser is the check.
- R3: token invisibility and the hidden chat are display: a console on the players' or the Spectator's browser reads
  what Foundry sends. Say it in the handbook rather than promise more.
- R4: LiveKit listen-only depends on avclient-livekit's private API (as E78 did).
