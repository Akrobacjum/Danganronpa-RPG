## 1. Goal

The owner's audit, stage E61 (`goal`), translated:

> The start of the trial has its own entry: a short sequence (the Stained Glass curtain closes over the school, the
> Class Trial title, the move to the hall from E13), and the verdict's announcement has a cut-in in Danganronpa's style
> (the accused's portrait, the vote's result, the verdict's word). Each player can skip either sequence on their own
> screen, both have a static version under reduced motion, and both start from one phase event on every client, the
> late one included.

Why (audit `why`): the owner's request of 26.09 for attractive elements, earlier outside the plan as new features; the
audit rates attractiveness about 6.05 today and about 7.8 after the plan, and these two are among the new elements
needed to go higher.

Done when (audit `doneWhen`, translated): "The Class Trial's start and the verdict have their sequences in both themes
and languages, with skip and a reduced-motion version, covered by the screenshot review and E53's budget. Floor
cross-section (12.10): a setting cross-section / floor switcher, cross-section by default; other floors show only the
discovered rooms under fog, without tokens (the rule of 09.10)."

Verify (audit `verify`): an R test that each sequence follows from one phase event, changes no world state, has a static
version under reduced motion and a working skip; a scenario of the trial's start and the verdict on four clients, each
getting each sequence once and nothing leaking before the verdict; screenshots in both themes and languages; live, the
sequence's time and the frame rate on the sandbox.

Risk (audit `risk`, translated): a full-screen animation's cost with 6-8 players and on weaker machines (E53 measures it
in the budget); the sequence starts from the phase event, not a client's clock, and never blocks or delays the phase
change; the cut-in shows only what the verdict announces anyway, never votes before the close; a reload mid-sequence.

## 3. What exists now (read at f22a7e8)

### 3.1 The phase event on every client

- The clock is a world setting (settings.mjs:2926, `onChange: () => onWorldChange(SETTINGS.clock)`, :2935). Its one
  writer is clock.mjs `setClock` (:117), inside `serialWrite(CLOCK_KEY)`; a move across the trial's edge stamps
  `timeOfDayStartedAt` with the server's clock (`serverNow`, clock.mjs comment at the trial edge).
- Every client runs the clock's row of the sync bus (sync.mjs:474). `clockNews` (:343) compares with what this browser
  last heard; `drpgPhaseChanged` is fired from the row when the phase moved (`phaseHook`, :450). Before the first run
  `heard` is null and every part counts as moved (:347): read, not run - whether that first run happens at `ready` on
  a client that loads during a trial (which would fire `drpgPhaseChanged` with no change at all) is to be measured in
  C1's red test.
- Today's listeners of `drpgPhaseChanged`: a11y.mjs:309, day-summary.mjs:71, live.mjs:67. None animates.
- `reconcilePhase` (clock.mjs:828) runs only on the GM that wrote the phase; its last step is trial-scene.mjs
  `followPhase` (:644), which activates the hall, so every browser's canvas is drawn again some time after its own
  `drpgPhaseChanged`. That delay is not measured anywhere in the harness (it has no canvas); LIVE-E13-01/-02 are the
  table's.
- Every road into the trial reaches this one event: Start the Class Trial (trial-floor-ui.mjs:92, its write at :190, its
  card "trialBegins" after it at :197-201), `setPhase` (clock.mjs, `startFloor` among its callers), Edit campaign, the
  API's `setClock`.

### 3.2 The curtain today on a phase change

- hud.mjs `renderHud` writes `data-drpg-phase` on the body when it changes (hud.mjs:495-521); stained-glass.css:265-267
  map the phase to `--drpg-glass-accent` (`classTrial` is `--drpg-sg-despair`).

  `rebuild` -> `rebuildAll` (:4273) -> `glassTurn` (:4103) with the old panes and look: today the entry into the trial

  reading for other files; `applyReducedMotion` (:178) sets the class for the Look window's switch or the system's.
- No closed-curtain geometry exists: `git grep -n -i "closed curtain\|curtain clos"` over scripts/glass.mjs,
  audit/glass-harness.html and audit/glass-sweep.mjs finds nothing. The mockup's "overlapping shards, scaled by E23"

### 3.3 The verdict

- verdict.mjs `applyVerdict` (:402) -> `runVerdict` (:545) runs `VERDICT_STEPS`; the card step `verdictCard` (:729)
  posts `postVerdictCard` (:676) once per verdict (`verdictCardPosted`, :735, accepts only a GM's message). The card is
  public by design: the sentenced's names, "executionPending" in the trial (nobody dies at the verdict since E70 C4,
  DC36), the right or wrong line, and flags `verdictCard` (chapter) and `verdictAt`. It names no Blackened.
- The vote's count is public before the verdict: vote.mjs `closeRound` (:1020) writes `accused`, `total`, `tied`,
  `majority` into `trialProgress` (a world setting; world-secrets.mjs:109 forbids only `mutedDead` outside the trial)
  and posts the count card (:1090) with `sfx: { key: "verdict", gm: true }`. So the existing `verdict` sound row
  (config.mjs:4928, "The verdict") plays at the vote's close, not at the verdict's card. The cut-in's new slot must not
  be confused with it (Q-E61-7).
- The Final Trial's verdict is a separate road (`applyFinalVerdict`, :1110; `finalVerdictCard`, :1246). Not in the
  owner's variants (Q-E61-4).
- The execution's own card and sound are at the trial's end (trial-progress.mjs:193, :227 `sfx: "execution"`).
