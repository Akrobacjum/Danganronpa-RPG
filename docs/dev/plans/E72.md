## 1. Goal

The audit's stage E72 (`goal`, translated). Its own first line: "One stage made of two (the owner, 30.10). Part A is
the former E67, part B the former E72." Part A: "the Monocub stops being a body and gets states the module counts by
itself, and the living stop hearing an ordinary dead student." Part B: "the Monocub gets two payers and a bet, and
every move is a row of `MONOCUB.abilities` with a resolver on the GM's computer."

Done when (`doneWhen`, translated, shortened): no Monocub state on the character but the `monocub` flag; the witness
mark, the invitation, the attempt counters, the dead's permission state and the move log live in GM stores, with an
owner's copy where the player must see them; a Monocub's move is paid on the GM after its conditions, and a refusal
costs nothing; no dead student finds a body; "your case is open" and the crime witness count themselves, traps
included; Redeem once a day, Ask for Hope settled by one GM; the invitation needs the Monocub Rule accepted, both roads
in the Students window go through it; an ordinary dead student is heard by nobody living from the end of the scene of
their death, and their BROADCAST_AUDIO changes only when the death is known or at a trial's start (a known-leaks row
and a README paragraph); no state changes a Monocub's voice (a tier-0 guard); both handbooks, the brochure and the
invitation say the Rule and its three steps. Part B: every move (Confusion on a student or a project, Redeem, Ask for
Hope, Ghost Bet) and both new Calls (Bribe, Bounty) are a row with a GM-side resolver, a test and a handbook line; no
secret project in any list or refusal; a bribe leaves in world data only the payer's Hope drop, its cards reach only
payer and Monocub; a Bounty leaves only a deferred Despair drop, its cards reach only Monocubs and GMs; a Monocub's
Hope has one reader and one spending road; no name in the data after a wrong verdict.
