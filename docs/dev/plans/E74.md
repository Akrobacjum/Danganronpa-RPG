# E74 - Doors and Keep Watch in the Infiltrate tile (1.2.114)

Plan written 12.11.2026 by the planning line, on the coordinator's release line at commit f22a7e8 (1.2.105 in progress). Documents only.

Lighter plan. **Depends on E69 (1.2.113):** the Infiltrate tile, the `INFILTRATE` table under `ACTIONS.listen.variants`
and the primary's `crossingSettled` event are E69's (docs/dev/plans/E69.md, C1 and C2). E74 adds four rows to that
table (Shut the Door, Force the Door, Pick the Lock, Keep Watch), two listeners of the event (the owner's warning and
Keep Watch) and a project preset. If E69's C2 slips, E74's C4 and C6 cannot start.

## 1. Goal

The audit's `goal` (stage E74), translated and shortened:

> The door actions and Keep Watch as further rows of E69's Infiltrate tile (DA9, DA22, DA25, DA28: all in one tile, no
> groups), and repairing a lock as a project preset (DA28), with no new tile and no change to the grid; the Rest tile
> stays as it is (DA38, DA48). Shut the Door (Body; a barricade as strong as the total, `maxExits: 2`), Force the Door
> (Body; a barricade by a contest with its strength, a lock and a seal at 18; `noise: "adjacent"`; a broken lock stays
> broken until repaired), Pick the Lock (Hand 15 opens, 18+ cleanly; a Shadow 16 cover; a pass for the picker alone,
> valid until they leave that room), Repair the Lock as a Craft preset at Trivial, a "Broken" column in Room Setup, and
> Keep Watch (Body, declared before an Eclipse, live cards of who is in, enters and leaves the post's room).

Done when (audit `doneWhen`): the four actions work in the Infiltrate tile per DA9, DA22, DA25 and the answers to
DA28-DA31, DA34, DA43, DA44, DA47, DA48 and DA52; Repair the Lock is a Craft preset at Trivial; the Rest tile and the
10-tile grid do not change; no surface reveals a barricade; the GM sends back every entry without a right; the state of
doors and watches is in the GM store, in the secret-field rules and in the season registry; E53's ledger records
barricades, breaks, picks with the Remnant's visibility, repair projects, owner warnings and watches; both handbooks
describe the actions, locks, the seal, and that a player who agreed to be a victim does not use Keep Watch to avoid it.

## 6. Risks

- The ambush in the victim's bedroom is intended (DA31 odds 43-58% to open); the counterweights (warning, Remnant,
  witnesses, banging, Keep Watch) must each have a test, since a missing one shifts the balance silently.
- The barricade must not leak: the window, the door list, the passages' glow, the refusal text and the room tile show
  it only after a pull on the handle. The public flag is the one accepted leak (DA47).
- The watch die changes the opening's roll: a missing `expectedFor` or `LEGAL_ROLL_MODIFIERS` row flags an honest roll.
- A room of several regions and Levels: flags and neighbours always through rooms.mjs.
- A Repair the Lock project must never exist without a broken lock, nor twice for one door.
- The `restrictedFrom` split touches the move veto every student move passes through; 66-rooms and 76-passages are
  the regression net.
