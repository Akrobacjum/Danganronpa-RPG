## 2. What exists now (read at f22a7e8)

- **The day in the clock.** One session is one day of five times of day (scripts/clock.mjs:6). `clockAfter` (:354)
  answers `rolledOver` when the next time of day is the first, and moves `session` and `day` with it; `advanceTimeOfDay`
  (:283) is the move. No separate "start the day" control was found (`git grep -i "startDay\|startSession\|beginSession"
  scripts` outside tests: nothing). Inferred: "the GM starts the day" is the Night-to-Morning roll-over. Q-E94-2.
- **The per-time-of-day summary.** day-summary.mjs (256 lines) already shows, on every browser, a summary at each end
  of a time of day (`registerDaySummary` :66, `sumUp`, `summaryWindow` :111, `showDaySummary` :187). Its rows come from
  this browser's private-card store (secret.mjs `secretSummaries`), so a player's rows are their own
  (`entries.filter(... isOwner)` in `showDaySummary`), and a browser whose clock is hidden for an incident shows none
  (`clockHiddenHere`). The end-of-day board can reuse this window logic over the whole day.
- **Overflow is a GM's number.** `overflowCount` answers 0 on a player's client (scripts/overflow.mjs:258, :266, "a GM's
  reading; 0 on a player's client (E05 C12)"). A "school mood from the Overflow counter" on a player's board therefore
  needs a coarse band the GM computes and sends, or it tells players a number the GMs keep. Q-E94-3.
- **Despair** is shown to players on the HUD's Despair panel (hud.mjs `matchStripToDespair`); Hope is each player's own.
- **Motion and sound.** motion.mjs `reducedMotion` (:135), `play` (:230); popup.mjs `showPopup` (:853); the Sound
  window's rows come from `SFX_CATEGORIES` (config.mjs:4488) and sound-panel.mjs. E61's sequence engine and its table
  switch do not exist at this commit (E61 is planned for 1.2.107); E94 builds on whatever E61 ships.
- **Notice history**: notice-history.mjs (`createNoticeEngine` :428, `noticeSnapshot` :405) keeps cards per browser.
- Scenarios that read this code: 40-flow (`secretSummaries`, `advanceTimeOfDay`), 61-gmstore-case (`advanceTimeOfDay`,
  overflow), 73-notice-history and the six others naming notice-history, 86-frame-work (E88's counts). None names
  day-summary.mjs.

## 3. Commits

**C0 - spec and red tests.** Read MK-R1/MK-R2; the acceptance table. Red: player A's end board holds player B's action;
it holds a secret event (a death the GMs keep, a secret project); the start board does not start on a late joiner's
browser; reduced motion still animates; the Overflow number reaches a player. R numbers: about 6.

**C1 - the day's window and the GM's line.** `dayWindow` beside `summaryWindow` (pure, R-tested): from the day's first
time of day to the roll-over, once per browser. The morning line: a GM field in Edit campaign or at the advance
(gm-campaign.mjs), stored where every player may read it once published (world-secrets.mjs rule: it is public after the
start, empty before). R: about 3.

**C2 - the start board.** A board through ui-kit.mjs (no new window kind outside it), on every connected browser at the
roll-over and on a late joiner's `ready` if the day started less than a set time ago (Q-E94-4); the cast from
`studentActors` with the dead as the table knows them (`isDeceased`, never `isDeadForGm`: a death the GMs keep must not
show as dead). Esc closes; static under `reducedMotion`; the E61 switch hides it. Lang keys EN and PL, after
docs/text-style.md and the glossary. R: about 5. Screenshots: both themes, 1920, 1280, 800 wide, reduced motion.

**C3 - the end board.** Own actions from `secretSummaries` over the day; public events from public cards only; Hope
counted up from the player's own sheet, Despair from the HUD's public reading; the mood band (C4). Stays until closed,
then into the notice history as one card. R: about 5. Scenarios: 40-flow, 73-notice-history.

**C4 - the mood band.** The primary GM maps the count to one of a few words (thresholds a fraction of X) and publishes the
word only; a rule in world-secrets.mjs that the count is never in it. R: about 3. Scenario: 72-canary (no count on a
player's browser after the end of a day).

**C5 - sounds, cost and the Spectator.** Two sound slots in `SFX_CATEGORIES` and the Sound window; 86-frame-work extended
with a board open (counts at rest within E88's budget); a Spectator (E92) sees the start board and no end board of its
own. Handbooks EN and PL. R: about 3.

Total new R numbers: about 25. `touches`: ["sockets"] if C4 sends the band by socket, otherwise null.
