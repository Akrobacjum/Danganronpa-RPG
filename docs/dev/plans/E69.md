## 1. Goal

The audit's `goal` (stage E69), translated and shortened:

> The Listen tile stays under the key `listen` and gets the label Infiltrate (DA27, English in Polish too), a glyph from
> E48's glyph map, a hint naming every action of the tile (after E74 seven rows: Listen, Tail Someone, Dash, Shut the
> Door, Force the Door, Pick the Lock, Keep Watch) and a list of variants from a new table `INFILTRATE` in config.mjs,
> reachable from `ACTIONS.listen.variants`. The window has one list of rows without groups (DA28); the first usable row is
> selected by default. One event of a settled crossing on the primary GM (S18-28): `crossingSettled { actorId, from, to,
> via, eclipse }`, emitted only once the token has stood in the new room about 2 s without being put back; it skips the
> GM's moves, put-back entries and the hall's tokens. Tail Someone (Shadow 15 decides whether the target notices; the
> effect on Leg or Shadow by proposal), live cards to the tailer, also in every Eclipse; Dash (Leg; 0-3 free crossings
> through `grantFreeMoves`), which loses a tail whose effect total is below the Dash's.

Done when (audit `doneWhen`): the Infiltrate tile has one list of rows without groups, Tail Someone and Dash work per
DA8, DA19-DA21, DA26 and the answers to DA27, DA28, DA32, DA33 and DA42, every notice of another character's move comes
from the one settled-crossing event on the primary GM, E53's ledger records tails per player per day, tails lost, Dash
uses and murders not opened because of a second person, and both handbooks describe the tile, Tail Someone and Dash.

## 6. Risks

- Live Eclipse cards reveal the target's rooms (DA20, intended). The tests must hold that no card names anyone else.
- The 2 s window can let a fast put-back through; a test puts the token back at 1.9 s.
- A silent end on death must never turn into a late card; cards are not queued when no GM is online.
- The eavesdrop depends on LiveKit behaviour nothing headless can measure (the harness has no audio).
- Murder privacy, if kept here, raises the stage from the audit's 2.75-3.25 effort by about half again.
- E69 does not have E39 and E40 to lean on; its own phase checks must be removed or merged when they arrive.
