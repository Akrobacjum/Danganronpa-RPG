## 1. Goal

The owner's audit, stage E97 (`goal`, Polish, translated):

> First a round of mockups for the whole trio E97-E99, shown to the owner before code. Then the GM builds the
> chapter's case between sessions in one window, "Case": the schema's store on the GM alone (GmStore); a graph in
> SVG with columns the module lays out and an order within a column that can be set (by hand with arrows, by kind,
> by room, by state, or so that the lines do not cross); editing of deductions, counters, minigames and the
> solution; a check of the case's consistency. The realization has a preview and does nothing until the GM clicks
> "Run realization" (it creates Key Remnants in the plan's slots, adds descriptions to existing Remnants, asks for
> Incident Remnants); everything it creates gets a marker, and "Undo realization" removes only what the players
> have not found yet. At "Body found" the GM gets a small window asking about the realization (nothing happens by
> itself), and the list of Incident Remnants still to place is in the GM panel.

Two later amendments change that sentence: the mockup round is already done (the 09.11 proposal page, amend of
10.11), and there is no separate "Case" window - the schema is the Map view of the Investigation panel (Q9, corrected
by the owner on 11.11).

Done when (audit `doneWhen`, translated): the GM builds the chapter's case, checks its consistency, runs and undoes
the realization with a preview, and gets the question at "Body found"; none of it reaches a player's browser.

Verify (audit `verify`, translated): mockups approved before code; R tests red, then green: the schema only in the
GM store and never on a player; the realization changes nothing before the click, and undo leaves what players
already found; the window at Body found does nothing by itself; screenshots of the graph in both themes and both
languages; at a table, a GM builds and realizes a case (a LIVE check).

## 7. Risks

- Secrecy: the schema is the answer key in bulk. It lives only in a GM store, the map is built only on a GM, the
  `made` marker is a store row, and 72-canary scans for all three.
- The realization changes the world. Preview first, a marker per item, an undo limited to what nobody holds; the
  reinforced Key Remnants need the deletion road `removeRemnant` refuses, so a mistake there deletes clues for real.
- Two GMs: per-field merge covers edits; it does not stop two runs, hence Q-E97-5.
- The first SVG graph in the module: no headless layout, so the look rests on screenshots and LIVE-E97-01.
- Performance: a redraw of the whole SVG on every store write; E98 measures 60 Remnants, and E97's schema adds its
  nodes on top - not measured.
- Q2 makes a GM's author the killer's own trace for clean-up; a mis-set author changes play.
- The fixed two-row footer is a kit change; done for every window, it moves every footer in the module.
- C1, C3 and C4 may already exist elsewhere (amend 11.11); building them again here would waste the work.
