What can be drawn

One of these is drawn at random each time the overflow fires - never more than one. Untick anything you do not want in the hat.

  • The lights go further down: 1 fewer crossing in the Eclipse, and an Eclipse that would have let you start anywhere is pulled back to two rooms.
  • Things are not where they were: 1 fewer search token in every room.
  • Nobody gets as much done: 1 fewer action each, on top of anything Wounded already took.
  • No Hope is earned at all. Spending what you already hold still works.
  • No Hope Calls. Not one, by anybody.
  • You cannot find your way for free: no Free Move. Crossings still cost actions as usual.

One-off

These happen once, the moment the overflow fires, instead of lasting the time of day.

  • Everything the school owns gets worse at once: 1 durability off every item with more than one point left. Nothing breaks.
  • The building shakes and work is undone: every project still under way loses 1 progress.

None of these ever goes below one. A time of day with no actions, or a room with no search tokens, is not a harder game - it is a player with nothing to do until the clock moves.
