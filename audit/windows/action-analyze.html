Learn what a Truth Bullet really is. A miss locks it for you this chapter, and it stays in play as it was. Outside a Class Trial, you can ask the GM for a hint instead; a higher total gets a clearer one.

  • Costs 1 action. You have 3 actions.
  • Rolls: Head
  • You are in: Dorm A
  • Identifying a bullet, by how visible its trace was - of the crime: Obvious 12 · Evident 15 · Subtle 18 · Hidden 21; faint: Obvious 8 · Evident 12 · Subtle 15 · Hidden 18; Key and Final: Obvious 6 · Evident 9 · Subtle 12 · Hidden 15.
  • A hint: 14+ for a subtle one, 18+ for a direct one.
  • Finding a stash: 16+.
  • Finding a secret passage: 16+.
  • The GM judges this one. Your roll and your request go to them.
