Rolls and privacy

Every player roll is whispered to that player and the GMs, and a GM's roll to the GMs.
Every player and Assistant GM throws Dice So Nice dice in the look of one full Gamemaster, fixed the first time the game runs with it on, with no special effects but those a Gamemaster marks as global, and a change of their own goes back to it.
Players cannot touch the dice, the statistic, advantage, experiences or the bonus - those come from the action, a Hope Call or the GM.
Another player's sheet opens redacted: name, portrait, Health and what they are holding.
Hides other characters unless they stand in the same room.
Those move through play - spend an action, take a rest, earn a Level Up.
