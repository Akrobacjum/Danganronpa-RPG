    Judged on Academy - Floor 1. 1 scene holds rooms.

    Walls first, then a Region for each room, drawn to them with snapping on; a room may be drawn as several regions of one name. A region is a room only once a GM ticks “This is a room” for it in Room Setup (the Doors tab): a region nobody ticked, a passage (a region that teleports a token or changes its level) and a region a player drew are no rooms and never count as neighbours. Neighbours touch along a shared edge and never overlap. A doorway is just a gap in the walls, of any width. Two zones means two rooms. The check below reports overlaps, borders drawn off their walls and corners off the grid; it never edits your map.
