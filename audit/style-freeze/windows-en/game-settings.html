These are the whole table's settings: a change here changes the game for everybody.

Music and voice

Swaps the playlist when the Eclipse opens, a trial or investigation begins, the time of day turns, or the game goes on hold.
Every mapped room becomes its own LiveKit channel - you hear only whoever stands in the room with you.
On, the camera dock leaves every screen and the number on the voice tile's speaker shows how many others are in your channel, speaking or not.
On, every browser joins voice inside the Foundry window and never opens LiveKit's separate window or tab, and LiveKit's box for that disappears from everyone's settings, the GM's included.
Bends the speed of the busiest effects by a few per cent on each play, so the fortieth button press is not identical to the first.

Rules at the table

How many times one room can be searched in a single time of day.
A roll that lands with Despair feeds the pool of that student's Monokuma.
Moving inside a room is free.
When off, an open Frisk only asks: a refusal or no answer ends it.
On, a player's proposed statistic for a Search or a project without a statistic is approved without a GM's press, and the card in their thread says so.
What happens when a player's token is moved through a wall its character could not pass: a line in the Debug log, or the token put back where it stood, its player told and the GMs warned.
Hides Daggerheart's single shared Fear bar and gives every Monokuma a Despair pool of their own.

Rolls and privacy

Every player roll is whispered to that player and the GMs, and a GM's roll to the GMs.
Every player and Assistant GM throws Dice So Nice dice in the look of one full Gamemaster, fixed the first time the game runs with it on, with no special effects but those a Gamemaster marks as global, and a change of their own goes back to it.
Players cannot touch the dice, the statistic, advantage, experiences or the bonus - those come from the action, a Hope Call or the GM.
Another player's sheet opens redacted: name, portrait, Health and what they are holding.
Hides other characters unless they stand in the same room.
Those move through play - spend an action, take a rest, earn a Level Up.

GM tools and map

Lights the whole map and lets the module's room fog hide things: the room you are in is clear, rooms you have visited show through a veil, the rest is fogged.
On: a GM's Foundry sidebar and scene controls stand in two windows of the module's, Foundry tools and Map tools, opened from the button above the handbooks.

Keeps the isometric-perspective module out of token configuration windows, where it breaks editing.
