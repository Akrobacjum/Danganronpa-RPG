“No privacy” is for corridors, stairwells and anywhere nobody can talk privately. It changes only the room count - the ratio line above, the same row in Season setup and Pre-season checks; movement, searching, rests and fog are unaffected. Bedrooms are already excluded, by having an owner.

“Neighbours” is where a door overrules the map: list the rooms this one opens into, separated by commas. Two rooms are connected when either lists the other, both ways. A room with a list is connected to nothing else, and a room beside it cannot walk in unless one of the two lists the other. Left blank, a room's neighbours are the rooms without a list whose walls share an edge with its own (a corner is not enough), and every room that lists it. A room the lists and the walls leave with no neighbour has no door out. A room that has no list, is in no list and touches no other room, not even at a corner, is not checked: a crossing from it may go to any room.

A secret passage is one stretch of wall between two rooms that only the students who know it can walk through, at a door's price. To everybody else it is a wall. Each button here takes effect at once; Apply and Cancel do not change it.

Two clicks on a wall between two rooms of this floor. A tunnel or stairs: a click in each of two rooms, on one floor or two.

No secret passages on this map.

The Mastermind knows every secret passage and is not listed. Close keeps who knows it, and the passage is a wall to all of them until it is opened again. Delete forgets the passage and who knew it. Make public: every student knows the passage, and nobody is told; Make secret: only the students listed here know it again. Known again after each season reset. Adding a student here does not give them the passage now. A tunnel or stairs can go one way only: pick it under its Shape. A passage in a wall always goes both ways.
